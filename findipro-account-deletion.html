Account & Data Deletion Policy

Google Play Compliance

Overview

At FindiPro, we respect your privacy and provide full control over your personal data. In compliance with Google Play Store policies and international data protection laws (including GDPR and CCPA), FindiPro users can permanently delete their account and all associated personal data at any time.

Method 1: Delete Directly in the FindiPro App (Instant)

You can delete your account and personal data instantly from within the Android application:

  1. Open the FindiPro app on your device.
  2. Log in to your account if you are not already signed in.
  3. Tap the Profile tab in the bottom navigation bar.
  4. Tap the Settings icon (gear icon) at the top right.
  5. Scroll down to the Danger Zone section.
  6. Tap Delete Account.
  7. Confirm your decision when prompted.

Upon confirmation, your Firebase authentication record is immediately deleted, your Supabase profile is purged/anonymized, and your device push tokens are deactivated.

What Data Is Permanently Deleted?

When an account deletion request is finalized, the following categories of data are permanently and irreversibly removed from our servers:

Data Category Specific Items Deleted
Account Credentials Firebase Authentication UID, email address, password hash, Google Sign-in association tokens.
Profile Data Full name, phone number, bio, avatar profile pictures, role (client/provider).
Provider Listings Business names, service categories, pricing information, skills, years of experience.
Media & Portfolio All portfolio images uploaded by service providers, National ID verification photos.
Communications In-app chat history, direct messages, sent images, and notification history.
Device & Push Tokens FCM push notification tokens, device identifiers, and presence/online tracking records.
Location Information GPS coordinates, saved service addresses, and geographic mapping entries.

Data Retention Exceptions (What We May Keep & Why)

Statutory & Security Retention

In strict accordance with applicable laws, certain limited records may be retained temporarily:

  • Financial & Transaction Records: If you engaged in paid subscriptions or bookings, basic transactional logs (amounts, dates, references) are retained for up to 7 years strictly to comply with tax, statutory accounting, and anti-fraud regulations.
  • Dispute & Abuse Prevention: Anonymized cryptographic hashes may be kept for up to 90 days to prevent banned bad actors from immediately recreating fraudulent accounts.
